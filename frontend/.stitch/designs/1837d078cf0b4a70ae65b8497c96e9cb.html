# Project Brief: ExamAI ## 1. Project Overview **ExamAI** is a clean, minimal, and AI-powered exam preparation platform designed specifically for college students and educators. The platform bridges the gap between course materials and student understanding through automated study aid generation (flashcards, quizzes) and an AI-driven chat interface that leverages teacher-provided context. ## 2. Target Audience * **Students:** College students looking for structured, subject-specific study tools and instant clarification on complex topics. * **Teachers/Professors:** Educators who need to manage course resources, monitor student progress, and provide AI-augmented study materials for their specific subjects. ## 3. Design Philosophy (Academic Clarity) * **Visual Style:** Minimalist, clean, and professional with "light playful edtech accents." * **Typography:** Geist font family (Clean, modern sans-serif). * **Color Palette:** * **Primary:** #3525CD (Deep Indigo/Violet) * **Background:** #f7f9fb (Light blue-grey) * **Surface:** White cards with subtle shadows. * **Geometry:** Soft rounded corners (16-24px radius) and full-rounded pills/buttons. * **Iconography:** Material Symbols Outlined. ## 4. Key Features - Student Experience * **AI Chat & Q&A:** A context-aware chat interface where students can ask questions about specific subjects. * *Features:* Subject switcher, inline citations [1] linking to source materials, and a "Materials Scope" panel to manage AI context. * **Subject Overview:** A central hub for each course (e.g., Data Structures) showing teacher avatars, progress stats, and a list of available quizzes and materials. * **AI Flashcards:** * *Library:* A deck management screen with mastery tracking (progress rings) and a "Generate New Deck" flow. * *Study Mode:* A minimalist, focused interface for flipping cards and self-assessment ("Still Learning" vs "Got It"). * **Quizzes:** Interactive assessment tools with detailed results pages showing weak topics and accuracy heatmaps. ## 5. Key Features - Teacher Experience * **Refined Dashboard:** A high-level overview of class performance, active students, and recent student activity across assigned subjects. * **Resource Management:** A collaborative library where teachers can upload materials (PDF, DOCX, etc.) and view resources shared by colleagues. * **Quiz Analytics:** * **Integrated Layout:** In-depth analysis of class performance including Grade Distribution, Weak Topics, and a Question Accuracy Heatmap. * **Mission Control (Dark Mode Variant):** A high-focus analytical view for tracking "Detailed Telemetry" of exam results. * **Quiz Creator:** A tool for generating new assessment modules from uploaded materials. ## 6. Information Architecture * **Student Sidebar:** Home, Chat, Quizzes, Flashcards, Resources. * **Teacher Sidebar:** Dashboard, Assignments, Question Bank, Analytics, Student Progress. ## 7. Future Roadmap * **Mobile Experience:** Native-feel responsive layouts for on-the-go studying. * **Peer Collaboration:** Group study modes and shared flashcard decks. * **Predictive Analytics:** AI-driven insights to warn students about "at-risk" topics before exams.
